## Decision

No independently meaningful local structural adoption is established. Reference has long paired lanes, low integrated rail attachments and readable left-wire handoff. Stable closeup shows the short inward-bending IN, disconnected-looking large cap/shaft hardware, exposed transverse ramp support and heel/rail overlap. Closed rail rendering could improve representation but does not solve these dominant relationships. Do not present another tiny collar or underboard-only detail as reference completion.

## Why hidden-volume fastening is not a sufficient substitute

A shaft/nut below floor top .04 could be made unreachable to legal above-floor balls, subject to board holes and physical load-path verification. It would be mostly invisible in the played view and leave all26 exposed upper caps/shafts unresolved. Adding a socket inside an already solid cap also leaves the same external silhouette and contact mismatch. Neither meets this task’s requirement for a meaningful major structural improvement.
