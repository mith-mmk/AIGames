## Finding

Do not adopt as reference improvement. The real bat-through-rail defect is relieved, but the pronounced double-curved lower paths and outward heel tails do not reproduce the reference's long, relatively straight IN/OUT bands. This is not a reference CAD dimension claim. Actual 45-degree, top and mobile390 views make the new serpentine shape conspicuous. Labels remain short, tilted patches; the static left-save cam crosses the OUT lettering. The narrowed OUT tail and large bend make route readability less direct. Upper S, wire, broad slings, robot print and board proportions remain unchanged. Replacing visible old caps with slender shafts does not by itself complete mounting construction.

The reference was actually inspected at artifacts/reference/current-reference.png. Accepted and candidate installed45/10/top/mobile/closeup screenshots were produced with the same view settings. Side and underside images were also preserved. The low installed side view is occluded by the cabinet side and cannot demonstrate clearance; it is not a successful unobstructed component view. Supplemental actual-mesh cutaways are explicitly labelled and cannot substitute for installed views.
