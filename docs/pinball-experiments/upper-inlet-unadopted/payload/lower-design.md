1. Preserve the accepted final guide/heel geometry and wire entry region from aboutz3.0 downstream. Do not defer the extra .40 inward shift to the last bat approach as the rejected candidate did.
2. Bring the longer upstream IN divider from roughlyx2.30,z2.2 toward its accepted x1.90,z3.0 before that region, using a continuous tangent and finite bend radius. This is an endpoint proposal, not an implemented or sphere-qualified curve.
3. Derive the OUT outer boundary from the SAME upstream curve and real normal passage width, merging into accepted outerx2.33 aroundz3.0 and existing outletx2.05,z4.88. Do not independently translate the outside rail or draw a curved stripe as a substitute.

5. Converging the OUT outer rail leaves a widening region between it and the straight shooter/cabinet. It must not become a blind external pocket or an unexplained third route. The minimal coupled study therefore also needs actual board-edge/drain/apron treatment: an explicitly modeled open collection region outside the converging guide, or a real formed perimeter structure.
