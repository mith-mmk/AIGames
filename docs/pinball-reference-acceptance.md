# ROBOBUDDY reference acceptance — 2026-11-13

**Status: in progress, not complete.** Reference likeness, mechanical construction and normal game play are all required. An intermediate image or a passing isolated fixture is not a replacement for these gates. The supplied reference remains private review input, not a distributed game asset.

## Reference-driven redesign

- The target composition uses three yellow wrench standups at upper left, two at middle left, and three red robot standups at middle right. The older left mini-bumper pocket has been removed. Each target has its own face, bezel, support, hinge, return spring, hit ID and contact-driven deflection. Display dimensions and contact boxes share the same definition.
- The right sheet ramp is being rebuilt as a continuous S, with an actual paired-wire return along the left edge. Its rendered floor, walls and physical contacts use the same sampled route. The wire section has two running rails and two containment rails, not an invisible supporting floor. Supports and joints remain visible.
- The lower section is redesigned together: rounded divider paths, separate IN and OUT passages, short broad slings, inward flipper pivots and new tapered bats. Each sling has a rounded silver frame, dark cover, gold side skirt, three orange corner lenses, posts, rubber, kicker, spring and linkage. Display and contact routes use shared parameters.
- Flipper shot angle now varies continuously with actual contact position and bat angle. The former discontinuity at contact position 0.4 was removed. No target location, ramp location or desired trajectory is used to aim a shot.
- The board carries a large silver robot with cyan face and heart, a peace-sign hand, blue city print and five lower mission indicators. These are registered inks, not collision barriers. The drain remains an actual opening.
- The cabinet has a supported live-score backglass, speakers, a rear silver tank, metal rails and a broad V apron with a gold collection grille below the deck. Both OUT routes and the central throat remain open.
- The former workshop room is replaced by a blue-purple city terrace with shaped building profiles, framed windows, braces, rails and a supported skybridge. Chappy is on a supported left rear pedestal and is no longer concealed by the backbox in the tested mobile view.
- Camera comparison uses 32° and 42° FOV at 45°, 48°, 50° and 56° elevation. The selected default is 32° FOV / 45° elevation, preserving the adjustable 10–90° range and saved view. No nonuniform scale is applied to the table or ball.

## Evidence rules

1. A standalone component or mounting-fixture image does not prove actual playfield clearance. Every primary component needs top, 45-degree, side and actual installed views. Moving mechanisms also need a contact-driven action check.
2. Physical incoming-ball fixtures explicitly set an initial position and velocity, then use Rapier collision, scoring and animation. They must not inject a hit or score. Natural launch and real-control tests are separate gates.
3. A complete ramp shot must start at the flipper, pass the ordered entrance/rear/exit gates, score once, return through the IN route, be recaptured and be fired again on the same ball without resetting its position. Entrance-only fixtures do not satisfy this gate.
4. OUT routes must naturally drain when uncharged. An earned kickback can use its normal one-shot mechanism. There are no unconditional side saves, hidden supporting floors or position-reset rescues.
5. Desktop and mobile viewport checks are automated Chrome checks. Physical-phone GPU performance and human difficulty evaluation remain unverified unless explicitly tested.
6. All dimensions and mechanical proportions are table-local estimates unless separately sourced in `pinball-mechanical-rebuild.md`. This is not manufacturer CAD or a strength-certified assembly.

## Current acceptance gates

This is a functional checkpoint, **not reference approval**. The working tree passed the full repository `npm test` command on 2026-11-13; the pinball part reported **130/130** tests (the added straight-tube geometry regression subsequently brings the pinball run to **131/131**), with three existing CSS baseline warnings and no lint errors. The browser render-budget gate and final smoke checks also pass after the geometry optimization below.

- **Reference likeness: not passed.** The right red target bank no longer covers the robot face, but the transparent ramp still makes an overlong upper arch instead of ending near upper center, and its right S is too shallow. The lower composition still has shorter lane bands, smaller slings/bats and less visual density than the reference. The shared dark target-bank carrier is still absent. These are open work, not accepted simplifications.
- **Real ramp route: passed at this checkpoint.** Five consecutive real-contact release delays, 78–82 simulation ticks at 120 Hz (650–683 ms), completed the ordered entrance/rear/exit gates, scored exactly one ramp traversal, returned through the actual left IN, settled on the held flipper and fired again without repositioning the ball. The browser additionally used keyboard release at 650 ms and verified the same-ball capture/refire.
- **Independent entry and rollback coverage:** Six measured ground entries at speeds 28/30 and lateral offsets −0.05/0/+0.05 traverse. The former oval-route tangent fixtures at speeds 26/28 and offsets −0.08/0/+0.08 now produce four traversals and two normal rollbacks (speed 26 at ±0.08). The latter climb the S, return through its entrance, drain normally and allow the next ball to launch. The old expectation that all six traverse is deliberately not claimed; this classification is separate from the real-flipper reachability gate. No hidden floor, trajectory correction or live position reset was introduced.
- **Natural launch and low-clearance regression: passed.** Twenty-one launch conditions clear the target/ramp throat, with no target-bank stall or live position reset. Earlier wider-ramp/common-frame candidates that trapped balls were rejected rather than hidden by a rescue rule.
- **IN / OUT: functional checks passed, reference shape still open.** Incoming IN fixtures at multiple speeds contact the held flipper; uncharged OUT fixtures drain without a flipper. The charged left kickback follows its OUT axis and returns the same ball upfield, using one charge. Its previous lateral kick into the divider was corrected. These results do not approve the remaining short and bent visual lane layout.
- **Installed contact and movement: passed.** Physical incoming-ball fixtures cover all three pops, two slings and eight standups. Each produces its expected collision ID, score and mechanical deflection. Target rendered extents match their shared collision definitions. These explicit incoming fixtures are not represented as unscripted full-game tests.
- **Keyboard / touch: passed.** Both keyboard flippers and mobile touch hold/release send the same ball into the upper field and score. The 367 ms release fixture is centered in the measured 40–48-tick stable window on both sides. It replaces the older 500 ms fixture whose new shot direction hits a nearer target. Controls and incoming state are not silently auto-aimed.
- **Other browser checks: passed.** Plunger power and weak return, nudge/TILT, multiplier and feedback/audio API tests pass. These do not establish sound quality by listening or human difficulty.
- **Multiple views: captured, not blanket approval.** All eight component types have top/45/side/mounting/action captures; cabinet glass, trough and underside have separately labeled inspection/cutaway views. Installed target/pop/sling views cover the actual current layout. Desktop at 10/45/56/90 degrees and mobile at 45/56/90 show the board; mobile 45 shows Chappy and score, with no horizontal overflow. Real phone hardware remains untested.

The revised scene initially exceeded the existing render budget (666,281 rendered triangles at desktop 45 degrees versus a 500,000 limit). Straight two-point structural tubes unnecessarily had 100 axial subdivisions. Reducing only those straight sections to one axial span preserves their endpoints, radius, eight-sided cross section and all components; curved tubing is unchanged. Desktop 45 now measures **357,401 triangles / 675 draw calls**, and mobile 56 **356,001 / 641**. The original budget is unchanged. Fresh browser smoke passes natural three-ball game over, input, pause, restart, mobile layout, blur handling, context restoration and reload. Before/after images retain the supports and silhouette. Reference-quality work remains open.

## Remaining scope limits

Targets are standups, not drop targets. Logical drain/serve is retained; powered trough-to-shooter transfer is not modeled. Glass exists but is hidden during ordinary play. Printed MULTI BALL / EXTRA BALL / JACKPOT labels do not imply those unimplemented rules exist. Real-phone performance and unscripted human playability have not been established.
